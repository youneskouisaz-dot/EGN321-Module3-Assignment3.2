# Capstone Suitability Checklist

## Candidate Workbook
Inventory List workbook

## Scoring

### 1. Real Operational Purpose — 2/2
The workbook supports a real inventory-management process involving stock monitoring, inventory value, and reorder decisions.

### 2. Real User — 2/2
A store manager, inventory manager, purchasing employee, or stock-control employee could use the workbook.

### 3. Meaningful Logic — 2/2
The workbook contains formula-driven inventory calculations and reorder-related logic.

### 4. Clear Inputs and Outputs — 2/2
Inputs such as unit price, quantity in stock, reorder level, reorder time, and discontinued status are identifiable. Outputs such as inventory value and reorder indicators are also identifiable.

### 5. Room for Improvement — 2/2
The workbook could be improved through better validation, clearer reorder logic, stronger documentation, and more transparent decision rules.

### 6. Suitable Complexity — 2/2
The workbook contains enough logic to support engineering analysis without being too large or difficult to understand.

### 7. Testable Logic — 2/2
Inventory value calculations and reorder conditions can be tested against expected results.

### 8. Safe for Classroom Use — 2/2
The workbook contains generic example inventory data and no confidential operational information.

### 9. Realistic Scope — 2/2
The workbook is small enough to analyze, improve, test, and document during the remaining course.

### 10. Handoff Potential — 2/2
The workbook could be improved so that another user can understand and maintain it more easily.

## Total Score

**20 / 20**

## Suitability Decision

The workbook is a strong capstone candidate because it has a clear operational purpose, identifiable users, meaningful formulas and decision logic, multiple areas for improvement, and a realistic scope for the remainder of the course.

The workbook should be suitable for continued analysis and improvement as the capstone project.
